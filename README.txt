ANIMATION STUDIOS - Version 1.4
===============================
A frame-by-frame 2D animation app for laptop and iPad (Apple Pencil).
No internet needed - just open index.html (keep logo.png, icon.png and apple-touch-icon.png in the same folder) (Chrome, Edge or Safari).
Works on GitHub Pages too: upload all files as they are.

START
Gallery -> "+ New animation" (choose canvas size, speed and paper) or open the Demo bouncing ball.
Your animations save automatically on this device/browser.

DRAWING TOOLS (left)
Pencil, Ink, Brush, Marker, Eraser, Fill, Shapes, Select, Picker, Move.
- Tap the active tool again for its settings (size, opacity, smoothing, pressure...).
- Apple Pencil pressure changes line thickness. Smoothing makes lines cleaner.
- Hold the pen still at the end of a line -> it snaps STRAIGHT.
- Fill: tap inside a closed shape. "Use lines from all layers" lets you fill on a colour layer under your lines.
- Shapes: circle, rectangle, line (hold Shift or hold still for perfect shapes).
- Select: lasso or box. Drag to move, corners to resize, side handles to SQUASH & STRETCH,
  orange dot to rotate. Flip, Copy, Delete, Done in the bar at the top.
- Colour circle: colour wheel, hex code, recent colours, saved swatches (+ Save).
- Size and Opacity sliders under the colour circle.

FRAMES (bottom bar 1, 2, 3, 4...)
- + = new blank frame, the copy button = duplicate frame.
- Tap a frame to go to it. Tap the current frame again (or long-press / right-click) for:
  duplicate, insert before/after, HOLD (keep on screen longer), move, clear, delete.
- Laptop: drag frames to reorder.

ONION SKIN: the onion button. Red = frames before, green = frames after (settings in the small arrow).
LAYERS: up to 3 (e.g. sketch, lines, colour) - show/hide, lock, opacity, move up/down.
PLAY: play button + fps (1-24). Fullscreen preview button next to it.
EXPORT: Video (MP4/WebM), GIF, or the current frame as PNG.

IPAD GESTURES
Pinch = zoom/rotate, 2-finger tap = undo, 3-finger tap = redo.
Once the Apple Pencil is used, fingers only move/zoom (palm rejection). Change in ... > Finger drawing.

LAPTOP SHORTCUTS
N Pencil, P Ink, B Brush, M Marker, E Eraser, G Fill, U Shapes, S Select, I Picker, H Move
[ ] size, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo, , . previous/next frame, A new frame, D duplicate,
Enter play, O onion skin, Space+drag move canvas, mouse wheel zoom, 0 fit to screen.

NEW IN 1.1 / 1.2
- TUTORIAL: tap "▶ Tutorial" in the gallery. Sticky the stick person talks you through
  9 steps (tap / Next to skip ahead), then plays a short ANIME ENDING.
- VOICE (1.2): Sticky reads every step aloud with the device's built-in voice
  (iPad/Mac/Windows voices). His mouth moves while he talks, and the next step starts
  after he finishes. 🔊 = voice on/off, 🗣 = choose voice + speed (saved).
  voice.mp3 is no longer used - you can delete it.
- SHARE: Export > "Share" sends your video / GIF / PNG through the iPad or laptop share menu
  (AirDrop, Messages, email, Teams...). If sharing isn't supported, it saves instead.
- Bouncing-ball demo is still there.

Coming later: auto in-between frames, sound, more layers.
